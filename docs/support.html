Getting started

  1. Open Workdoro and follow the short tour to meet everyone in town.
  2. Tap + to give Pip a task, like "call mom friday 6pm" or "gym every monday".
  3. Visit Bea's café to focus, Mo's library for notes and Juniper's greenhouse for habits. Everything you do wakes the town up a little more.

Common questions

My town isn't on my other iPhone or iPad. Make sure both are signed in to the same iCloud account and that iCloud Drive is on. Settings in Workdoro shows the sync status.

The focus alarm didn't ring. Allow notifications for Workdoro in your iPhone Settings, and check Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing it.

How do I add a widget? Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Workdoro.

How do I start over? Settings → Delete all data. Export first if you want a copy.

Does it cost anything? Workdoro is free. No ads, no tracking, no account.
