Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Workdoro is built to keep your things private. In short: your things stay on your devices and in your own iCloud, and we never see them. The one exception is the Together House, and only if you choose to go in.

What the app stores

Your tasks (letters), notes, drawings, photos you add to notes, habits, focus sessions and town progress are saved on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, a copy is also kept in your own private iCloud Drive so your town appears on your other devices; only you can access it. Your widgets read a small summary of them so they can show your day.

What we collect

Only what you choose to share in the Together House, described below. Workdoro has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your tasks, notes, habits and everything else are never sent to us or to anyone else.

The Together House

The Together House is an optional shared room where people around the world hang out, eat and work together. If you go in, Workdoro shares these things with everyone inside: the name you type, the character you pick, where your character is in the room and which seat or snack it has, the emotes and one-tap messages you send (chosen from a fixed list, so there is no free text), whether you are focusing or on a break, with the time that block ends, and how many shared sessions in a row you have finished (shown as a crown for the longest run). Nothing else is shared: no email, no location, no contacts and nothing from your tasks or notes.

This information is kept in Workdoro's public database in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). Apple gives the app an anonymous ID for you instead of your Apple Account, so your seat isn't linked to who you are. Your record is deleted when you leave. If the app closes without leaving, you disappear from the room after about a minute, or when your focus block ends.

If you report someone, the report stores their name and ID so it can be reviewed, and names reported by several people are hidden for everyone. Hiding someone only happens on your own device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Workdoro schedules reminders and focus alarms on your device. They are never sent through a server.

Photos

When you add a photo to a note, only that photo is copied into the app. Workdoro cannot see the rest of your library.

Your control

You can export all your data or delete it at any time in Settings. Delete all data also removes the iCloud copy and removes you from the Together House. You can turn off iCloud for Workdoro in your iPhone Settings at any time.

Children

Workdoro is not directed at children under 13, and the only information it handles about anyone is what they choose to share in the Together House.
