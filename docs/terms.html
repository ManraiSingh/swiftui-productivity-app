Using Workdoro

Workdoro is a personal productivity app for your tasks, notes, focus sessions and habits. You can use it for free on your own devices for any lawful purpose.

Your content

Everything you write, draw or add stays yours. It is stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. We do not receive, read or own any of it.

Backups

You are responsible for keeping copies of anything important. Settings → Export my data saves everything to a file you can keep.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes everything from the app and from its iCloud copy. This cannot be undone.

Availability

Workdoro is provided as it is. We work to keep it reliable, but we can't promise it will always be free of mistakes or available on every device.
