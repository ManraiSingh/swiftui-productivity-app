## Description (4000 max)
```
Welcome to Workdoro, a sleepy pixel town that wakes up as you get things done.

Every building is a tool, and every tool has a friend who runs it. Finish your tasks, write your notes, focus at the café and water your habits, and watch the town come back to life.

📮 TASKS AT THE POST OFFICE
Give Pip your to-dos and he carries them as letters. Type naturally, like "call mom friday 6pm" or "gym every monday 7am", and Pip works out the date, time and repeat. Every letter you deliver helps him find a piece of his broken bike.

📚 NOTES AT THE LIBRARY
Mo keeps your notes as pages of her book. Write, draw with pens, add photos, sticky notes, shapes, checklists and templates for journals, meetings and brainstorms. Give a note as many pages as you need. Pin, sort into folders and search everything.

☕ FOCUS AT THE CAFÉ
Order a drink from Bea, pick a seat and work beside the regulars. Set your own rounds and breaks, like 25/5 or 50/10. On breaks, wander the café and chat with Bea and the locals. A gentle chime tells you when time's up. Strict mode turns your phone sideways into a quiet desk, where you can work on your laptop or curl up with a book.

🌱 HABITS IN THE GREENHOUSE
Every habit grows into a real plant. Water it each day and watch it sprout, bud and bloom. See your streaks, your best run and a calendar of every day you showed up. Forgot to mark one? Just tap it.

🍰 TREATS AT THE BAKERY
Focus sessions bake treats. Enjoy one on a break, gift one to a friend, or save three to rescue a broken streak.

🏛️ YOUR WEEK AT THE TOWN HALL
A cozy summary of everything you did, day by day.

✨ AND MORE
• Home screen widgets with your letters, café time and habit streaks
• Tick off tasks right from the widget
• Live Activity and Dynamic Island while you focus
• Day, sunset and night, daily weather and changing seasons
• iCloud sync across your iPhone and iPad
• A quick guided tour when you arrive

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No tracking. Your things stay on your device and in your own iCloud. We never see them.

Your little town is waiting. Come say hi.
```

## App Privacy ("Nutrition label")
In App Store Connect → App Privacy:
- **Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?** → **No, we do not collect data from this app.**

This is accurate: everything stays on the device and in the user's own iCloud, which Apple doesn't count as collection.

## Export compliance
- **Does your app use encryption?** → **No** (it only uses Apple's built-in HTTPS/iCloud).
  Already set in the app (`ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO`), so uploads won't ask.
